About these data

Each dot is one occurrence record in OBIS: a tag or receiver detection, a sighting, a catch, or a museum specimen. Positions of tagged sharks are often delayed or coarsened by the researchers who hold them.

The map starts with the last 12 months. Records often reach OBIS months or years after they are collected, so recent periods can look sparse. Choose a longer time range to see more.

When a species has more records than the plotting limit, the dots are an arbitrary subset. The density view always counts every matching record.

Each record keeps the licence of its source dataset. Follow the dataset link in a record's popup for citation details.
